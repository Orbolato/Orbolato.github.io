# Lucas Orbolato Carvalho

Civil-Aeronautical Engineer and researcher at the Aeronautics Institute of Technology (ITA), Brazil, working on air traffic flow and capacity management, reinforcement learning under uncertainty, runway operations, Urban Air Mobility, and probabilistic aviation analytics.

## Research areas

- Air Traffic Flow and Capacity Management (ATFM / ATFCM)
- Reinforcement learning for tactical and strategic demand-capacity balancing
- Runway capacity management under weather and operational uncertainty
- Urban Air Mobility, eVTOL operations, trajectory scheduling, and deconfliction
- Bayesian neural networks and probabilistic aviation prediction
- Reproducible machine learning for aviation
