Research focus

My current research develops operationally grounded machine-learning and reinforcement-learning methods for aviation systems in which demand, capacity, weather, and operational conditions evolve under uncertainty. A recurring objective is to connect data-driven methods with decisions that air traffic managers, airport operators, and future UAM service providers can interpret and evaluate.

Air Traffic Flow and Capacity Management
ATFM, ATFCM, miles-in-trail, tactical and strategic demand-capacity balancing.
Runway Capacity
Runway configuration, arrival-departure allocation, weather uncertainty, operational forecasting.
Urban Air Mobility
Vertiport networks, corridors, on-demand scheduling, eVTOL operations, emissions, and safety.
Probabilistic Aviation Analytics
Bayesian neural networks, stochastic simulation, risk analysis, and open machine learning.

SIGMA-Sky Project & Interactive Dashboards

SIGMA-Sky — Integrated Solutions for Air Mobility Management – Airside

SIGMA-Sky investigates new operational concepts, rules, and air traffic management services for the safe and efficient integration of Advanced Air Mobility (AAM) operations into the airspace. The project addresses airspace design, separation and conflict-management approaches, performance indicators, and computational simulation for quantitative evaluation of operational scenarios.

Profiles and citation guidance

Publication entries provide DOI and ResearchGate links when available, together with project, preprint, conference, code, and reproducibility links where relevant.
